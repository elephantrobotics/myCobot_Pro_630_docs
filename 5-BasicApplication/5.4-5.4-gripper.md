Gripper installation: <br>

- The adaptive gripper inserts the gripper onto the pin on the atom, as shown in the figure below: <br>
![](../resourse/6-ApplicationBaseRoboFlow/6.5/6.6_1.png)<br>

- The electric gripper is plugged into the 485 interface on the top, as shown in the figure below: <br>
![](../resourse/6-ApplicationBaseRoboFlow/6.5/6.6_2.png)<br>

(Note: When connecting the clamping jaws, pay attention to the groove at the connection point. It must be aligned correctly, as incorrect connection methods may damage the clamping jaws!!!)

## 1 Adaptive gripper

Supported devices: myCobot280, 320, 600 && myPalletizer 260
Function description: <br>

- Run RoboFlow, enter the program editing interface, and select **M5Stack-basic**--**Gripper** (for instructions on how to enter the program editing interface, please refer to **6.2 Simple Usage of RoboFlow**);<br>

**mycobot pro600 does not support hot plugging. The robotic arm must be powered off, meaning the end lights are not illuminated, before connecting the gripper. Plugging or unplugging while the system is powered on poses a risk of damaging the gripper. **
- IO mode (Tool selection I/O, overall steps as shown in the figure):

  ![](../resourse/6-ApplicationBaseRoboFlow/6.5/6.6.3_3.gif)

  IO controls the opening and closing of the gripper, with pin tool_out0 closed and tool_out1 open, indicating that the gripper is open

  ![gripper open](../resourse/6-ApplicationBaseRoboFlow/6.5/6.6_4.png)

  IO controls the opening and closing of the gripper, with pin tool_out0 closed and tool_out1 open, indicating that the gripper is open

  ![gripper close](../resourse/6-ApplicationBaseRoboFlow/6.5/6.6_5.png)

- Click **Define New Gripper**, fill in the Gripper Name as "CAG-1" (this name is mandatory), and then click Save; <br>

- Transparent transmission mode (Saved status can be selected as open or close, and the overall steps are shown in the figure below):<br>

  (Note: Before using the transparent transmission mode, you need to set the gripper to transparent transmission mode, i.e., set_cag_gripper_mode(0). For detailed interface usage, please refer to [Socket API](https://docs.elephantrobotics.com/docs/pro600-cn/2-serialproduct/2.3-myCobot_Pro_600/2.3.5-Socket%20API%20InterfaceDescription.html))

  ![](../resourse/6-ApplicationBaseRoboFlow/6.5/6.6.1_3.gif)<br>





## 2 Electric gripper
Supported device: **myCobot320**
Function description: <br>

- Run RoboFlow and select **M5Stack-basic**--**Gripper**; <br>
- Click **Define New Gripper**, fill in the Gripper Name as "DH PGE 8" (this must be the exact name), and then click Save; <br>
- Select "open" or "close" for "Saved status". The overall steps are shown in the figure below: <br>
![](../resourse/6-ApplicationBaseRoboFlow/6.5/6.6.2_3.gif)<br>
